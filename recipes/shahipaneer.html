Shahi Paneer with Roti

Description of this dish:

Shahi paneer with roti is a royal North Indian meal. Soft cottage cheese cubes sit in a thick, velvety gravy made from cream, yogurt, and ground nuts. Warm, soft wheat flatbread scoops up the sweet and aromatic sauce.

Ingredients:

  • Paneer: 250 grams (cut into cubes)
  • Onions: 2 medium (sliced or chopped)
  • Tomatoes: 3 to 4 medium (pureed)
  • Cashew nuts: 10-12 (soaked in warm water for 15 mins)
  • Whole spices: 1 bay leaf, 2 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon, 2-3 green cardamoms
  • Ginger-garlic paste: 1 tablespoon
  • Whisked curd (yogurt): ½ cup
  • Heavy cream: 3-4 tablespoons
  • Spices: 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala, salt to taste
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves): 1 teaspoon
  • Ghee or butter: 2 tablespoons
  • Water: 1 cup
  • Whole wheat flour (atta): 2 cups
  • Water: As needed to knead
  • Salt: A pinch (optional)
  • Ghee or butter: For brushing

Steps to prepare this yummy dish:

  1. Heat 1 tbsp of ghee/butter in a pan. Add the whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon, cardamom).
  2. Add chopped onions and sauté until translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste and diced tomatoes.
  3. Cook until tomatoes turn mushy, then add soaked cashews. Cool and blend this mixture into a smooth paste.
  4. Heat the remaining ghee/butter in the same pan. Pour in the blended onion-tomato-cashew paste.
  5. Sauté on medium heat until oil separates from the edges.
  6. Lower the heat and mix in the turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt.
  7. Slowly add the whisked curd while stirring constantly to prevent curdling. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
  8. Pour in 1 cup of water and bring the gravy to a gentle simmer.
  9. Add the paneer cubes, crushed kasuri methi, and garam masala. Simmer for 3-5 minutes.
  10. Turn off the heat and stir in the fresh cream. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  11. Mix wheat flour and water to knead a soft dough. Rest it for 15 minutes.
  12. Divide into equal balls, roll out into flat circles, and cook on a hot tawa on both sides until golden brown spots appear. Puff them up over an open flame and brush with ghee.
