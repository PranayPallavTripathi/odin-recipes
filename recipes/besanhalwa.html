Besan Halwa

Description of this dish:

a classic, rich Indian sweet made by slow-roasting gram flour (chickpea flour) in pure ghee, then simmering it with sugar and milk or water. It has a soft, fudgy texture, a deep golden color, and a warm, nutty aroma enhanced by cardamom.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • ½ cup ghee
  • ¾ to 1 cup sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 cups water or milk (or a mix of both)
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts (almonds, cashews, pistachios)

Steps to prepare this dish:

The following are the steps to prepare this yummy dish:

  1. Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottom pan or kadai on low-medium heat.
  2. Add the besan and roast it continuously for 10 to 12 minutes.
  3. Keep the flame low so the flour does not burn. Stir constantly until it turns golden-brown and smells nutty.
  4. Warm your water or milk in a separate pan.
  5. Pour the warm liquid slowly into the roasted besan. Stir fast to stop lumps from forming.
  6. Add the sugar and cardamom powder.
  7. Stir well as the mixture thickens and leaves the sides of the pan.
  8. Garnish with chopped nuts and serve warm.
