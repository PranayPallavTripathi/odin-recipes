Poha

Description of this dish:

Poha is flattened rice flakes and a popular Indian savory breakfast dish made by steaming and sautéing the flakes with mustard seeds, turmeric, onions, and green chilies. It is light, fast to cook, and naturally gluten-free.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ cups thick or medium poha (flattened rice)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons raw peanuts
  • ¾ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • ¾ teaspoon cumin seeds (optional)
  • 8 to 10 fresh curry leaves
  • 1 to 2 green chilies (slit or chopped)
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 small potato (cubed small, optional)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice

Steps to prepare this yummy dish:

  1. Put the poha in a large strainer or colander.
  2. Rinse it gently under running tap water for a few seconds.
  3. Do not leave it standing in water or it will turn mushy.
  4. Sprinkle turmeric, salt, and sugar directly over the damp flakes.
  5. Toss gently and set aside to soften.
  6. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  7. Add the peanuts and fry until crunchy and golden.
  8. Remove the peanuts from the pan and set them aside.
  9. In the same remaining hot oil, add the mustard seeds and cumin seeds.
  10. Let the mustard seeds pop.
  11. Add the curry leaves and green chilies.
  12. Add the chopped onions and diced potatoes.
  13. Cook until the onions are soft and the potatoes are tender (cover the pan for 3 minutes if using raw potatoes).
  14. Add the softened, seasoned poha into the pan.
  15. Mix everything together very gently so the flakes do not break.
  16. Cover the pan with a lid and let it steam on low heat for 2 to 3 minutes.
  17. Turn off the heat.
  18. Stir in the fried peanuts, fresh coriander leaves, and fresh lemon juice.
  19. Mix one last time, check for salt, and serve warm.
