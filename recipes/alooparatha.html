Aloo Paratha with dahi

Description of this dish:

Aloo paratha with curd is a famous North Indian comfort meal. It features a hot, golden-brown whole wheat flatbread stuffed with spiced mashed potatoes, cooked crisp with ghee, and paired with a bowl of cool, creamy, and tangy yogurt (curd).

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • Water as required for kneading
  • 3 to 4 medium potatoes (boiled, peeled, and thoroughly mashed)
  • 1 to 2 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger or ginger paste
  • 1 tablespoon fresh coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon dry mango powder (amchur) or lemon juice for tanginess
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chili powder or Kashmiri chili powder
  • Salt (to taste)

Steps to prepare this dish:

  1. Knead the dough: Mix 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta), 0.25 tsp salt, 1 tbsp oil, and 0.5 cup water. Knead into a soft, smooth dough, cover it, and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Prepare the potato filling: Boil and peel 3 medium potatoes, then mash them completely until smooth with no lumps.
  3. Add spices: Mix the mashed potatoes with finely chopped green chilies, 0.5 tsp red chili powder, 0.5 tsp garam masala, dry mango powder (amchur), salt to taste, and fresh coriander leaves.
  4. Stuff the dough: Take a small ball of dough, flatten it slightly, and place a spoonful of potato filling in the center. Bring the edges together, seal it tightly at the top, and gently round it into a ball.
  5. Roll out the paratha: Dust the ball with dry flour and roll it out carefully into a 6-inch flat circle, taking care not to press too hard so the filling stays inside.
  6. Cook on the tawa: Place the rolled paratha on a hot griddle (tawa) over medium-high heat. Cook for about 30 seconds until small bubbles form, then flip it.
  7. Add ghee or oil: Spread 1 tsp of ghee or oil on the cooked side, flip again, and press the edges gently with a spatula until golden brown spots appear on both sides. Serve hot with curd or pickle.
